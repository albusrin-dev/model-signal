Disclosures

Last updated: October 2026

Model Signal is committed to editorial independence. This page explains how we handle commercial relationships so you can evaluate our work with clear eyes.

Editorial independence

Sponsored content and affiliate relationships do not determine what we cover or the conclusions we reach on unrelated topics. We do not grant partners approval rights over editorial judgments outside the specific, disclosed integration.

Sponsorships

When a video or written piece includes a paid integration, it is clearly disclosed — both spoken (in video) and visual. Integrations are written in our voice and separated from the core editorial analysis. Partners may review only for factual accuracy about their product and for contracted legal requirements.

Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. When material, we disclose this near the link and in the video. Recommendations are based on audience value, not commission size. We explain limitations and alternatives when relevant. We never rank a product higher because it pays more.

No guaranteed reviews

We do not accept arrangements that require a positive review or that hide the commercial nature of the relationship. We reject fake urgency, deceptive earnings claims, and products that fail a basic reputation and safety check.

Opinions and advice

Content on Model Signal reflects our analysis and opinions at the time of publication. It does not constitute investment, legal, tax, or professional advice. You should do your own research and consult qualified professionals where appropriate.

Updates

We may update this page as our practices or legal requirements evolve. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
