Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Model Signal collects, how we use it, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as necessary and never sell personal data.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter email: If you subscribe, we store the email address you provide and basic subscription metadata (sign-up date, confirmation status).
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics that collect aggregated, non-identifying information such as page views, referrers, and approximate geography. We prefer tools that do not require cookies for basic measurement.
  • Server logs: Standard server logs may include IP address, user agent, and request timestamps for security and debugging. These are retained only as long as needed for those purposes.

How we use information

  • To send the newsletter you requested and related service messages (e.g. confirmation, unsubscribe).
  • To understand aggregate traffic patterns and improve the site.
  • To protect the site against abuse and ensure reliability.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your email address or other personal data.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not build detailed behavioral profiles of individual readers.

Third-party services

Newsletter delivery is handled by an email service provider. Analytics, if used, are provided by a privacy-focused analytics provider. YouTube embeds (on the Watch page) are subject to YouTube’s own privacy practices. We choose providers with an eye toward minimizing unnecessary data collection.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time via the link in every email.
  • Request deletion of your email address from our list by contacting us.
  • Use browser settings to limit or block analytics and third-party content.
