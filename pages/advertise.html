Partnerships

Work with Model Signal

We accept a small number of relevant sponsorships and affiliate relationships. Audience trust is the asset. Revenue never overrides editorial judgment.

What we accept

  • Products relevant to creators, developers, founders, and professionals evaluating AI tools
  • Claims that can be independently verified
  • Clear disclosure accepted by the partner
  • Full editorial independence retained by Model Signal
  • Category and company that pass a reputation and risk check
  • Documented compensation and deliverables

What we reject

  • Guaranteed positive reviews
  • Undisclosed integrations
  • Fake urgency or deceptive earnings claims
  • Unsafe or low-reputation products
  • Approval rights over unrelated editorial conclusions

How it works

  1. We evaluate fit, conflicts, claims, and reputation.
  2. We define deliverables, dates, usage rights, revision limits, exclusivity, cancellation, and payment.
  3. We independently verify product claims.
  4. We write a clearly separated integration in the channel’s voice.
  5. Sponsor review is limited to factual/product accuracy and contracted legal needs.
  6. Spoken and visual disclosure is displayed.
  7. We track audience response, conversions, complaints, and long-term trust.

Affiliates

When we recommend tools we actually use or have independently evaluated, we disclose clearly near the link and in the video when material. We recommend based on audience value, not commission size. We explain limitations and alternatives when relevant. We never rank a product higher because it pays more.

Get in touch

Interested? Email partnerships@modelsignal.example with product details, proposed timing, and any relevant materials. We reply to serious inquiries that fit the standards above.
